How Agamos works

From "we're engaged" to "thank you" — three easy steps for the couple, one for every guest.

1

Build your wish list

Add gifts from any store, the Agamos catalog, or set cash goals like a honeymoon or home deposit.

2

Share your page

Send one beautiful link. Guests browse your story, see what's needed, and pick a gift in seconds.

3

Receive & redeem

Guests buy outright or chip in toward big goals. Withdraw cash or redeem items — then say thank you.
